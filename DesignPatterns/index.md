# Design Pattern Catalogue

[← Back to main index](../index.md)

Every institution has recurring dysfunctions and, for each one, a structural counter-design that prevents it. This catalogue pairs the two: 42 antipatterns, each traced to the mechanism that produces it, each paired with the design pattern that is its structural inverse. Thirty of the 42 pairs were identified directly inside expert dialogues about higher education governance (Blocks 1–5); the remaining twelve (Blocks 6–7) were derived afterward from principles those same dialogues kept circling, without being named outright at the time — a real distinction, not a formality, and it's flagged again where it matters.

> Each entry consists of a paired antipattern and design pattern. The antipattern is the recurring dysfunction observed in practice: named, described in structural terms, and traced to the mechanism that produces it. The design pattern is its structural inverse: what an institution must build so that the antipattern cannot arise. The two are paired deliberately — the design pattern is not a generic best practice but a specific structural response to the specific structural failure documented alongside it.
>
> The catalogue follows the convention established in software engineering by the Gang of Four (Gamma et al., 1994) and extended by Fowler's refactoring catalogue: each entry is structured consistently so that it can be read as a reference, cited independently, and applied to new institutional contexts. The entries are derived strictly from documented observation. They do not represent ideals or aspirations — they represent the structural inverse of documented dysfunction.

This maps directly onto the maturity signature in [Part 5 — Maturity Signature](../part5-maturity-signature.md) *(placeholder — link once that document has a page)*, which scores the OUG's own governance architecture against every one of these 42 pairs.

## A Note on Blocks 6 and 7

Blocks 6 and 7 (pairs 31–42) were not identified inside a dialogue the way pairs 1–30 were. They were derived afterward from principles that two expert dialogues — referred to in the source material as "Person 1" and "Person 2" — kept circling without ever being named outright. The book's own framing: *"These pairs emerged from the act of asking: given this principle, what is the structural failure mode it addresses, and what is its structural inverse?"* Both derivation paths are treated as legitimate. The distinction is preserved here so a reader knows which pairs carry the additional weight of having been independently identified in dialogue, and which were derived by extension.
