## Strategy Architecture

* [ ] **Strategy Framework / SEP** — the source document `Strategy Framework (Objectives & KPIs).docx` exists but has not yet been converted into the Strategy directory. It provides the missing connection between the Strategic Constitution and the operational OKR framework by defining strategic objectives, indicators, responsibilities, and the annual strategic review process.

## Founding and Legal Model

* [ ] **Legal form and founding pathway** — the [Five-Year Finance Plan](five-year-finance-plan.md) describes the OUG as beginning as a non-profit company (`gGmbH`) and later becoming a privately operated, state-recognised distance university. The [Grundordnung](../governance/grundordnung.md) defines the OUG as a university of the State of Baden-Württemberg. These models must be reconciled or clearly presented as alternative founding pathways.

* [ ] **Academic staffing model** — the Five-Year Finance Plan assumes that the OUG directly employs professors for accreditation, academic oversight, and doctoral education. Other parts of the OUG architecture describe a federated model in which teaching capacity and affiliated academic staff are primarily provided by partner institutions. The intended staffing model and any minimum direct-employment requirements need to be stated consistently.

## Financial Model

* [ ] **Internal consistency of the Five-Year Finance Plan** — the Executive Summary, detailed profit-and-loss tables, cash-flow tables, and explanatory text contain different figures for annual results, cumulative deficits, operational break-even, and financial sustainability. The calculations and narrative conclusions must be reconciled before the plan is used externally.

* [ ] **Finance Plan and workbook reconciliation** — the [Five-Year Finance Plan](five-year-finance-plan.md) and [OUG Finanzmodell v3.xlsx](OUG%20Finanzmodell%20v3.xlsx) should be checked against one another so that student numbers, ECTS volumes, revenue assumptions, staffing, operating expenditure, capital expenditure, grant funding, cash requirements, and break-even figures correspond.

* [ ] **ECTS price and partner compensation** — the Five-Year Finance Plan fixes a price of €25 per ECTS and a 60/40 division between the OUG and teaching partners. The partnership architecture instead provides for ECTS-based remuneration through a published Tariff Schedule. The relationship between the fixed finance-plan assumptions and the tariff-based partnership model must be clarified.

* [ ] **Funding assumptions** — the Five-Year Finance Plan relies on a €3 million BMBF startup grant, founding capital, further bridge financing, ancillary income, and rapid student and partner growth. These should be clearly identified as planning assumptions or scenarios unless and until the relevant funding commitments and implementation conditions exist.

## Referenced Foundations

* [ ] **European HEI Digital Alliance Framework Documents 1–6** — the Five-Year Finance Plan repeatedly identifies these documents as the academic and technical foundation of the model, but they are not currently represented in the repository. The references should either be mapped to existing OUG documents, supported by the original source documents, or removed.
