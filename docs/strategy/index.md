This domain answers two separate questions that most institutional strategy documents blur together: what does the OUG believe, unconditionally, that no amount of operational pressure should be allowed to change — and what does the OUG need financially to survive long enough for those beliefs to matter? The Strategic Constitution answers the first question with genuine rigor (explicit trade-off precedence rules, not just aspirational principles). The Five-Year Finance Plan answers the second with uncomfortable honesty — including a phased pilot design that names, in advance, what happens if the federated model doesn't work.

## What's Here

* **[Strategic Constitution](strategic-constitution.md)** — the "meta-strategy": five First Principles, five derived capability domains, five Strategic Pillars, and — the part that makes this more than a values statement — explicit precedence rules for when they conflict. Includes a formal A/B Strategic Decision Protocol for testing real decisions against the constitution.

* **[The OUG Role Statement](role-statement.md)** — not a mission statement. A falsifiable claim about what gap the OUG fills that no other institution fills, reviewed every three years specifically to check whether it is still true. Makes the constitutional argument, not just the market argument, for why the state has an obligation here.

* **[Five-Year Finance Plan 2026–2030](five-year-finance-plan.md)** — full P&L, cash flow, and IT cost breakdown, plus the Phased Founding and Decision Gate Framework: a three-partner pilot with named OKR thresholds and a Year 2 Gate Review that can produce four outcomes, including an honest "Wind Down" path with a learner-protection protocol.

* **[OUG Finanzmodell v3.xlsx](OUG%20Finanzmodell%20v3.xlsx)** — the downloadable financial workbook supporting the detailed assumptions and calculations behind the financial planning in this domain.

* **[Strategy Formation Protocol](strategy-formation-protocol.md)** — how strategy actually gets built, not just approved: a seven-phase, up-to-18-month participatory process with explicit safeguards against predetermined outcomes dressed up as consultation.

* **[OKR Framework & Governance Dashboard](okr-framework-dashboard.md)** — the transformation-oriented counterpart to quality monitoring. Four-layer OKR cascade, a four-panel dashboard visible to the whole governance community, and the Bug Protocol for treating missed targets as institutional learning rather than blame.

* **[Ministeriumsmemorandum](ministeriumsmemorandum-v2.md)** — the direct case made to the Baden-Württemberg ministry, in German: why the OUG is intended to operate through the existing higher-education framework and why its establishment is presented as an institutional and governance decision rather than a conventional programme proposal.

## Connections to Other Domains

* **[Governance](../governance/index.md)** translates the Strategic Constitution into authority, decision rights, institutional bodies, escalation routes, and safeguards.

* **[Quality](../quality/index.md)** complements the OKR framework. Strategic monitoring asks whether the OUG is moving in the intended direction; quality governance asks whether its academic work remains valid, coherent, and trustworthy.

* **[Curriculum](../curriculum/index.md)** translates strategic commitments to quality, access, adaptability, and learner agency into programme structures, pathways, modules, and assessment.

* **[Partnership](../partnership/index.md)** defines how the federated model, partner participation, teaching contributions, and ECTS-based compensation operate within the strategic and financial architecture.

* **[Communications](../communications/index.md)** translates the institutional case into forms suitable for public explanation, partner dialogue, and ministry engagement.

Together, these documents connect constitutional commitments to strategic choices, measurable development, financial viability, and a defined founding pathway.

Unresolved questions and matters requiring further reconciliation are maintained separately in the **[known gaps register](../strategy/known-gaps)**.
