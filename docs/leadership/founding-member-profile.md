## Relevance to the Open University of Germany

Prof. Berkling is the founding architect of the OUG. Her contribution to the institution is not advisory — it is constitutive. The New Study programme, which she built as the first state-recognised fully English-language online degree in Baden-Württemberg, is the OUG's first accredited programme and its proof-of-concept. The platform described in the OUG's Digital Platform Architecture document — the living learning system with eight developmental milestones — is her own technical creation. The governance architecture she has developed for the OUG across more than twenty institutional documents draws directly on her experience as an elected Senator in the DHBW system, as a founder of international special interest groups, and as a practitioner who has built distributed teams and federated educational partnerships across three continents.

Her research record provides the empirical grounding for the OUG's pedagogical commitments. The task-based learning study (Berkling, in review) — which demonstrated a near-perfect engagement-gain correlation (r = 0.958), the Productive Struggle Paradox, and the anti-ceiling effect — is the evidential foundation for the OUG's learning design principles. Her work on self-assessment, online learning readiness, and competency tracking informs the platform's progress visibility architecture. Her two-decade engagement with gamification and personalised learning is embedded in the competency-based structure of the B.Sc. CS programme.

> The OUG is not an idea Prof. Berkling is proposing. It is an institution she has already begun to build — module by module, document by document, partnership by partnership. The accreditation process formalises what is already operational.
