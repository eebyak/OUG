## Resolved

- [x] **"Governance Mentor System" gap closed.** Tracked in [known-gaps.md](../known-gaps.md) as a mechanism referenced elsewhere (the Leadership Development Framework itself references "Governance Mentor" throughout) but never located as its own standalone document. It isn't one — it's Section V of [`leadership-development-induction-framework.md`](leadership-development-induction-framework.md), "The Governance Mentor Pool." known-gaps.md updated to reflect this.

- [x] **Incentive Conversion Statement duplication resolved.** [`amendment-transformation-mandate.md`](amendment-transformation-mandate.md) (Amendment C) and [`governance/incentive-conversion-statement.md`](../governance/incentive-conversion-statement.md) described the same instrument at two stages of development. Per decision, kept the fuller governance version as canonical; the leadership amendment now points to it instead of repeating the full specification.

- [x] **Founding Council Letters anonymized.** The original draft named three real, living public figures (Kathleen Manning, Andreu Mas-Colell, Thomas Estermann) as letter recipients, including specific biographical claims attributed to them — one clearly invented ("you once described how the GSE nearly failed in Year 4…"). Per decision: all names removed, replaced with role-description placeholders (`[Name TBD]`), and the most identity-revealing specific achievement descriptions (e.g. "you built the Barcelona Graduate School of Economics") generalized so the letters read as role templates rather than letters to specific identifiable people. A "candidate search in progress" marker was added to both the document itself and [`index.md`](index.md), making clear no one has actually been approached yet.
