This page records documents, inconsistencies, or unresolved decisions that affect the Leadership and People domain.

Some items require the leadership documents to be reconciled with the Grundordnung and Governance architecture. Others concern terminology or implementing mechanisms that are referenced but not yet fully defined.

**How to use this list:** for each item, determine whether it can be resolved by correcting an existing document, clarifying the relationship between constitutional and leadership roles, or adding the required implementing provision.

---

## Rectorate Structure

- [ ] **Number and portfolios of Vice Rectors** — the [Leadership & Stewardship Profiles](leadership-stewardship-profiles.md) define three Vice Rector roles: Academic Quality and Curriculum Architecture, Learner Development and Access, and Institutional Learning and Innovation. The [Grundordnung](../governance/grundordnung.md) provides for two part-time Vice Rectors. The intended number of roles and the allocation of portfolios must be reconciled.

- [ ] **Rector term and renewal** — the [Amendment on the Rector's Transformation Mandate](amendment-transformation-mandate.md) specifies a four-year term renewable once. The [Grundordnung](../governance/grundordnung.md) provides for a term of six to eight years for full-time Rectorate members. One rule must be selected and used consistently across the leadership and constitutional documents.

- [ ] **Rector's constitutional role** — the Leadership & Stewardship Profiles describe the Rector as the “Constitutional and Strategic Steward.” The [Governance & Decision Framework](../governance/governance-architecture.md) assigns final authority for constitutional interpretation, conflict resolution, and revision to G1, while the Rectorate operates at G2. The profile should state unambiguously that the Rector safeguards and escalates constitutional questions but does not replace the final authority assigned to G1.

## Leadership Frameworks and Measures

- [ ] **Transformation Reserve source** — the Transformation Mandate states that the Rector's “Proactive Capacity allocation” is specified in the Leadership & Stewardship Profiles and reserves at least 20% of it for transformation. The current profile document contains no Proactive Capacity provision or percentage allocation. The reference must be corrected, or the underlying provision must be added to the appropriate leadership document.

- [ ] **PRECEPTA, DYNAMOS.v2, and PERSONA** — the Leadership & Stewardship Profiles establish PRECEPTA as a binding normative framework but refer to DYNAMOS.v2 and PERSONA only by name. Their relationship to the Leadership Development and Induction Framework, their authority, and whether they require separate definitions should be clarified. Terms that are not intended to become part of the institutional architecture should be removed rather than left as unexplained frameworks.

## Mission Hub Leadership

- [ ] **Dean and Steward of Missions terminology** — the Leadership & Stewardship Profiles use “Dean” and “Mission Hub Steward,” while the Grundordnung uses “Steward of Missions.” The documents should adopt one consistent role name or state clearly that these are the legal and constitutional names of the same office.

- [ ] **Mission Hub leadership eligibility** — the Grundordnung restricts election as Steward of Missions or deputy to professors employed full-time by the OUG. The leadership profile describes an elected peer steward but does not address how this requirement operates in a federation that relies substantially on partner-employed and affiliated academics. The eligibility rule and the intended federated staffing model must be reconciled.

## Document Status

- [ ] **Status and provisional closing section** — the Leadership & Stewardship Profiles currently presents PRECEPTA and the role profiles as binding interpretive references, while the document status itself remains unset. Its closing section on “Speed 1” and “Speed 2” is explicitly retained as a provisional working note. The document should distinguish clearly between normative provisions, explanatory material, and provisional design ideas before formal use.
