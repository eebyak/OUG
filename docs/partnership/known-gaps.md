This page records documents, inconsistencies, or unresolved decisions that affect the Partnership and Federation domain.

Some items require existing documents to be reconciled. Others concern implementing instruments that are referenced by the partnership architecture but are not yet available in the repository.

**How to use this list:** for each item, determine whether it can be resolved by correcting an existing document, mapping terminology to the current governance architecture, or adding the required contractual or operational instrument.

---

## Governance and Representation

* [ ] **Partner Senate participation** — the [Partner Membership Framework](partner-membership-framework.md) gives each partner an Academic Voice at the annual Senate Strategy Session but explicitly states that this person is a guest without voting rights. The [Federated Participation & Incentive Framework](federated-participation-incentive-framework.md) gives Programme Partners a Senate seat and Strategic Partners enhanced Senate representation. These provisions must be reconciled.

* [ ] **Governance terminology** — the Federated Participation & Incentive Framework assigns responsibilities to a Supervisory Board, Finance Committee, and Academic Senate. These terms are not mapped explicitly to the bodies and G1–G4 layers used in the current governance architecture. The responsible bodies for tariff setting, financial oversight, partner rights, and framework adoption must be named consistently.

## Contractual and Operational Instruments

* [ ] **Partnership Agreement and annexes** — the Partner Membership Framework is intended to form part of every Partnership Agreement and refers to Schedule A, Module Agreements, role confirmations, and partnership-specific escalation arrangements. The corresponding agreement template and annexes are not currently available in this directory.

* [ ] **Operational forms and templates** — the framework refers to a Partnership Expression of Interest form, Competency Mapping Template, Module Submission Form, quarterly Signal Report, Eligibility Assessment, and Full Partnership Confirmation. These instruments should be created or identified before the onboarding process can operate as described.

* [ ] **Partnership Office operating model** — both frameworks assign substantial responsibilities to the OUG Partnership Office, including onboarding, relationship management, classification of unassigned tasks, compensation administration, annual reviews, and partner development. Its mandate, staffing, authority, workflows, and service responsibilities have not yet been defined in a consolidated operating model.

## Finance and Exit

* [ ] **Tariff Schedule** — the Federated Participation & Incentive Framework bases partner remuneration on a published Tariff Schedule, including a Base Rate, Quality Premium, and Partnership Tier Supplement. No current Tariff Schedule has been identified, and the authority responsible for approving it depends on the unresolved governance terminology above.

* [ ] **Exit notice periods** — the Partner Membership Framework permits either party to exit with six months' notice. The Federated Participation & Incentive Framework specifies six months for Teaching Contributors and twelve months for Programme and Strategic Partners. The applicable notice periods and their relationship to learner-protection and teach-out obligations must be reconciled.

## Technical and Legal Implementation

* [ ] **Lehrpersonalmodell implementation** — the [Amendment on Lehrpersonalmodell and Kapazitätswirksamkeit](amendment-lehrpersonalmodell.md) defines three engagement models but also relies on implementing instruments, including a standard Lehrauftragsvertrag and a template Freistellungsvereinbarung. These templates and the required legal review have not yet been identified in the repository.
