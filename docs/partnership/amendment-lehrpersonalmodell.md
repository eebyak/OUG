> **Purpose of this amendment.** The Partner Membership Framework currently describes what partners do and how their contributions are governed. It does not address how teaching staff from partner institutions are legally engaged with the OUG — a gap identified in the [Rechtliche Implementierungsvoraussetzungen](../governance/legal-implementation-requirements.md) document (Rechtspunkt 3: Kapazitätswirksamkeit und Lehrpersonalmodell). Without this specification, the question of whether partner teaching staff are acting within their institutional deputat (kapazitätswirksam) or as independent contractors (Lehrauftrag) is unresolved at the partnership boundary. This amendment closes that gap by adding a new section on the Lehrpersonalmodell and integrating the Task Architecture as a formal annex.

## Amendment A — Lehrpersonalmodell: How Teaching Staff Are Engaged

*New Section 2a — to follow Section 2 (Partner Rights) in the Framework*

The OUG's federated model depends on teaching staff from partner institutions delivering OUG modules. How those staff are legally engaged with the OUG has significant implications for the partner institution (capacity obligations, employment law), for the individual (tax, pension, intellectual property), and for the OUG (beihilferechtliche Konformität, credential integrity). This section specifies the three permitted engagement models and the conditions under which each applies.

### Model A — Lehrauftrag (Standard Model)

The standard engagement model for teaching staff from partner institutions is the individual Lehrauftrag. Under this model, the staff member receives a Lehrauftrag from the OUG as a Nebentätigkeit (secondary activity). The Lehrauftrag is not kapazitätswirksam at the partner institution — it does not reduce the partner's official teaching capacity as calculated under the Kapazitätsverordnung (KapVO). It is subject to the Nebentätigkeitsgenehmigung of the partner institution and to any tarifliche Grenzen (collective agreement limits) on secondary employment, typically 8 Semesterwochenstunden.

Each Lehrauftrag is individually agreed, individually remunerated at a rate set in the Partnership Agreement, and individually subject to the OUG's module quality standards. The Lehrauftragnehmer (teaching contractor) holds the Urheberrecht (copyright) on materials they create — unless they assign it by contract. The standard OUG Lehrauftragsvertrag includes a Nutzungsrechtslizenz clause under which the OUG receives a permanent, non-exclusive licence to use, adapt, and share the materials within the federation. See also [Rechtliche Implementierungsvoraussetzungen](../governance/legal-implementation-requirements.md), Rechtspunkt 6.

### Model B — Freistellungsvereinbarung (Enhanced Model)

Where a staff member's engagement with the OUG is substantial enough that a single Lehrauftrag limit would be insufficient, the OUG and the partner institution may enter a Freistellungsvereinbarung. Under this model, the staff member is partially released from their institutional deputat to contribute to the OUG. The release is kapazitätswirksam at the partner institution — the partner's official capacity is reduced by the released proportion and must be compensated, either by the OUG or through an agreed credit mechanism.

Freistellungsvereinbarungen require the approval of the Ministerium für Wissenschaft, Forschung und Kunst BW (or the relevant Land ministry) and are subject to individual negotiation. The OUG Partnership Office maintains a template Freistellungsvereinbarung reviewed by the OUG's Hochschulrechtler. This model is used sparingly — only when the standard Lehrauftrag model cannot accommodate the scope of the staff member's contribution.

### Model C — Eigenes OUG-Lehrpersonal (OUG-Employed Staff)

For core functions — including the Curriculum Designer role, Design Authority membership, and Quality Commission membership — the OUG employs its own staff directly. These positions are kapazitätswirksam for the OUG. They are advertised, appointed through a competency-based process, and subject to OUG employment terms. OUG-employed staff hold their Urheberrecht on research outputs but assign curriculum design outputs to the OUG under their employment contract, consistent with § 43 UrhG.

### Governing Table: Lehrpersonalmodell by Engagement Type

*Quick reference for Partner Contacts and OUG Partnership Office*

| Dimension | Model A — Lehrauftrag | Model B — Freistellung | Model C — OUG-Employed |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Kapazitätswirksamkeit** | Not kapazitätswirksam at partner | Kapazitätswirksam — partner capacity reduced | Kapazitätswirksam at OUG |
| **Genehmigung required** | Nebentätigkeitsgenehmigung at partner institution | Ministerial approval required | OUG appointment process |
| **Remuneration** | Lehrauftragsvergütung per module | Agreed cost-sharing between OUG and partner | OUG salary (TVöD/TV-L equivalent) |
| **Urheberrecht** | Staff retains; OUG receives licence | Staff retains; OUG receives licence | OUG owns curriculum outputs (§ 43 UrhG) |
| **Typical use** | Standard module delivery | High-volume or multi-year engagement | Core governance, Design Authority, QA |
| **Volume limit** | Typically 8 SWS per semester | By agreement — no formal cap | No limit — employment contract governs |

## Amendment B — Task Architecture as Formal Annex

*Confirmation that Section 5 of the existing Framework constitutes the required Task Architecture annex*

The existing Section 5 of the Partner Membership Framework ([Task Architecture — Required Annex to Partnership Agreement](partner-membership-framework.md#section-5--task-architecture)) already constitutes the formal Task Architecture required by HP-7 (The Designed Task) and by the Gap Closure decision for that principle. This amendment makes that status explicit.

> **Formal Annex Status of Task Architecture**
> *Reason: HP-7 gap closure — Task Architecture must be a named governance document integrated into the partnership agreement*
>
> Section 5 of this Framework is formally designated as **OUG Task Architecture v1.0**. It is a required governance document, not an optional annex. Every Partnership Agreement must reference it by name and version. When the Task Architecture is revised, all existing Partnership Agreements are notified and given 60 days to acknowledge the revision. Any task that arises in the OUG/partner relationship and is not covered by Section 5 is treated as Unclassified and submitted to the Partnership Office for classification within 10 working days of arising. The classification decision is added to the Task Architecture in the next scheduled revision. Unclassified tasks may not be assigned informally — they must wait for classification or be escalated to the joint governance layer.
