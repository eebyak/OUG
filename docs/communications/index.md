This domain answers a question none of the others do: having built all this — the governance, the curriculum, the legal case, the finance model — how do you actually say it out loud, to six different audiences who each need to hear something different first? These documents range from the most rigorous (a full competitive market study, with real names and real numbers) to the most human (a fictional day in the life of the institution once it exists) — deliberately, because different audiences are moved by different kinds of evidence.

## What's Here

* **[The OUG Communication Architecture](communication-architecture.md)** — the governance document underneath all the others in this domain: four expression levels (30 seconds through full narrative) and six audience-specific adaptations, each naming what that audience needs to hear and what they are afraid of. Everything else here is downstream of this document's logic.

* **[Market Study](market-study.md)** — a genuinely rigorous competitive analysis against FernUniversität Hagen, EuroTeQ Engineering University, and IU International University, including an unflinching look at IU's real, publicly reported difficulties and what the OUG commits to doing differently.

* **[Ein gewöhnlicher Dienstag](ein-gewoehnlicher-dienstag.md)** (*A Perfectly Ordinary Tuesday*) — a fictional narrative following one day, three years after founding, across six people and six cities. Not marketing copy — the most concrete answer available to “what does this institution actually feel like once it is running?”

* **[OUG Report 2025–26](annual-report-2025-26.md)** — an earlier-stage strategic report documenting the funding-initiative portfolio (OPEN STUDY, OPEN PATHWAY, VIRTUAL STUDY, DIGITAL STUDY) that preceded the OUG's later, fuller institutional design. Useful as historical context for how the thinking evolved, not as a description of the current architecture.

## Presentations

The **[Partner & Ministry Decks](partner-and-ministry-decks/index.md)** directory contains five audience-specific PowerPoint presentations:

* **OUG Erklärung** — the general introduction;
* **OUG Die Fehlende Schicht** — the strategic positioning deck;
* **OUG Menschlicher Fall** — the human case for change;
* **OUG Hochschulpartner DHBW** — the university-partner presentation;
* **OUG Ministerium** — the ministry-facing case.

The decks translate the same institutional architecture into different starting points for public explanation, partner dialogue, and ministry engagement. Their underlying logic is described in the [Communication Architecture](communication-architecture.md), while the fuller written ministry case is available in the [Ministeriumsmemorandum](../strategy/ministeriumsmemorandum-v2.md).

Unresolved questions and matters requiring further reconciliation are maintained separately in the **[known gaps register](known-gaps.md)**.
