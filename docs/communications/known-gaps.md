This page records documents, inconsistencies, or unresolved decisions that affect the Presentation and Communication domain.

The communications documents translate the wider OUG architecture for public, institutional, partner, and ministry audiences. They therefore need to remain aligned with the authoritative documents in the Governance, Strategy, Partnership, Curriculum, and Quality domains.

**How to use this list:** correct communication documents when an underlying institutional decision changes. Do not resolve an architectural inconsistency only by changing its public wording.

---

## Communication Architecture

* [ ] **Institutional claims require reconciliation** — the [OUG Communication Architecture](communication-architecture.md) presents several planning assumptions as settled characteristics of the institution, including the legal founding pathway, the €25-per-ECTS price, the partner revenue share, the accreditation route, the break-even threshold, and the implementation timeline. These claims should be aligned with the authoritative Strategy, Finance, Governance, and Partnership documents before the Communication Architecture is used externally.

* [ ] **Document references** — the Communication Architecture refers to `OUG_Hagen_IU_PatternSignature_Alignment.docx` as part of the full institutional narrative, but that document is not currently available in the repository. It also refers to the Didactic Framework by its former `.docx` filename rather than linking to the current [Didactic Framework](../curriculum/didactic-framework-bsc-cs.md). The references should be corrected or the missing source recovered.

## Market Evidence

* [ ] **Verification and dating of external claims** — the [Market Study](market-study.md) and Communication Architecture contain quantitative and comparative claims about student numbers, prices, ownership, financial performance, programme structures, digital credentials, and competitor strategy. These claims should be supported by dated sources and reviewed before external publication because the underlying information can change.

* [ ] **OUG target claims in the Market Study** — the Market Study describes the OUG using specific assumptions about legal form, recognition route, pricing, student growth, digital infrastructure, credential technology, and financial sustainability. These should be reconciled with the current institutional and financial architecture and clearly identified as targets where they are not yet established facts.

## Presentations

* [ ] **Deck alignment review** — the five presentations in the [Partner & Ministry Decks](partner-and-ministry-decks/index.md) should be checked against the current repository before formal external use. In particular, their descriptions of legal form, public status, partner participation, pricing, financial assumptions, founding timeline, accreditation, and implementation readiness should match the authoritative documents.
