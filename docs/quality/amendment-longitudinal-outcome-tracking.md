## Amendment: Longitudinal Outcome Tracking — GP-3 Gap Closure

> **Purpose of this amendment.** The OUG Stress Test against GP-3 (Learning Is Measured by What Endures) found that the current quality architecture measures quality at the point of delivery and at module completion but does not track what learners can do at six and eighteen months after completion — the point at which durable, transferable learning becomes visible. The Stichprobe and Quality Commission operate at delivery time. The Developmental Audit Domain 2 asks whether Bloom-level progressions are being achieved in practice. Neither reaches beyond the credential issuance moment.
>
> This amendment adds a longitudinal outcome layer to the quality architecture. It does not replace any existing section — it adds new subsections to Section 3 (Assessment and Stichprobe) and Section 5 (Academic Quality Report), and amends the Developmental Audit Domain 2 reference. Together these additions close the GP-3 gap by ensuring the quality system can distinguish between institutions that produce compliant completion and institutions that produce durable capability.

## Amendment A — New Section 3.6: Longitudinal Outcome Indicators

*Reason: GP-3: Learning Is Measured by What Endures — quality must reach beyond credential issuance*

*Insert as Section 3.6 of the Quality Assurance & Academic Governance document, following Section 3.5 (Stichprobe).*

### 3.6 Longitudinal Outcome Indicators

The Stichprobe and Quality Commission assessment operate at the moment of delivery. They can determine whether a module was well-designed and well-delivered. They cannot determine whether the learning it produced is durable, transferable, and genuinely capability-building — the dimension of educational quality that matters most for the OUG's constitutional mission and for the learner whose credential is intended to open professional opportunities.

The OUG therefore maintains three longitudinal outcome indicators, tracked on defined cycles and reported in the Annual Academic Quality Report (Section 5). These indicators are not accreditation requirements. They are the quality architecture's honest answer to the question: do our learners develop the capabilities we claim to develop?

**LOI-1 — Six-Month Competency Self-Assessment.** At six months after completing any module, the OUG contacts the learner with a structured self-assessment survey. The survey is anchored to the specific competencies the completed module was designed to develop (as specified in the Competency Codebook, v10.0). The learner rates their current confidence and frequency of application for each targeted competency on a four-point scale. The survey also asks one open question: what from this module has proved useful in your professional or personal context?

The six-month survey data is aggregated by module, by Mission Hub, and by partner delivery site. Modules with consistently low self-assessed transfer receive a Quality Commission flag and are reviewed in the next Stichprobe cycle. Modules with consistently high transfer are documented as quality exemplars for curriculum design guidance.

**LOI-2 — Eighteen-Month Employer or Professional Context Feedback.** At eighteen months after completing three or more modules, the OUG invites learners to share employer or professional context feedback on the competencies developed. Where learners consent to employer contact, the OUG sends a brief structured questionnaire to the employer or professional supervisor. The questionnaire asks three questions: have you observed the learner applying competencies related to their OUG modules in their work? If yes, at what level of proficiency? If no, what competencies would have been most useful?

The eighteen-month feedback is not a graduate employment survey. It is a capability application survey — the question is not whether the learner is employed but whether the learning transferred to their context. Data is reported annually to the Quality Commission and the Senate as part of the Academic Quality Report.

**LOI-3 — Developmental Audit Domain 2 Longitudinal Evidence.** The [Developmental Audit Framework](developmental-audit-framework.md) Domain 2 Self-Assessment — Learning Quality and Credential Integrity — is amended to include the following standing question in every audit cycle:

> "What does the LOI-1 and LOI-2 data from the past three years tell us about whether learners who complete our modules are developing capabilities that endure and transfer? Where is the evidence strongest? Where is the gap between intended and actual capability development largest, and what structural explanation do we have for that gap?"

The Developmental Audit Peer Panel is given access to the full LOI dataset for the audit period. The Panel Assessment addresses whether the institution's interpretation of that data is credible and whether the development commitments that follow from it are adequate.

## Amendment B — Section 5, Annual Academic Quality Report: Add Longitudinal Reporting Requirement

*Reason: GP-3: quality reporting must include outcome evidence, not only delivery compliance*

*Add the following to the Annual Academic Quality Report specification in Section 5.*

The Annual Academic Quality Report includes a Longitudinal Outcome Section reporting the results of LOI-1 and LOI-2 for the reporting year. The section must include: the response rates for each indicator; the aggregated competency transfer ratings by module and by Mission Hub; the modules flagged for Quality Commission review based on low transfer ratings; and the quality exemplars identified based on high transfer ratings. The Longitudinal Outcome Section is presented to the Senate alongside the Stichprobe findings. The Senate's academic quality discussion must address both.

## Summary: Quality Architecture After Amendment

The quality architecture now operates across four temporal layers. Each addresses a different quality question and uses a different measurement instrument.

| Layer | Instrument | Timing | Quality question addressed |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Continuous** | Platform analytics + partner signal reports | Ongoing / quarterly | Is learning engagement happening at module level? |
| **At delivery** | Stichprobe + Quality Commission assessment | Annual (5% sample) | Is the module well-designed and well-delivered? |
| **At completion** | Competency assessment + Europass credential | On module completion | Has the learner demonstrated the targeted competency? |
| **Longitudinal** | LOI-1 (6 months) + LOI-2 (18 months) | Rolling, reported annually | Does the learning endure and transfer to real contexts? |
