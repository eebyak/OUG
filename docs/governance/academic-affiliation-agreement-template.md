## § 1 Parties

This Academic Affiliation Agreement ("Agreement") is concluded between the Open University of Germany (OUG) and **[Name of Academic]**, holding primary appointment at **[Home HEI]**.

## § 2 Status

(1) The affiliated academic holds primary employment at the Home HEI and a secondary academic affiliation with OUG.

(2) This affiliation does not constitute an employment relationship with OUG, and does not itself create statutory membership, electoral rights, or eligibility for OUG's statutory organs — those arise only where separately granted by the Grundordnung and applicable election regulations (Federated Legal Model § 8(5)).

## § 3 Duration

The affiliation is granted for a fixed term of **[X years]**, renewable subject to OUG approval, and contingent on continued primary appointment at the Home HEI.

## § 4 Rights

The affiliated academic has the right to:

- participate, with advisory voice, in the Community Circle (Grundordnung § 5 Absatz 14);
- contribute to curriculum and programme design through the Programme Development Forum;
- be nominated by their Mission Hub for examination board service (Degree and Examination Authority document);
- propose curriculum initiatives through their Mission Hub;
- access institutional academic resources required for OUG programme delivery.

**Where this Agreement is concluded under Engagement Model B (Freistellungsvereinbarung) rather than Model A (Lehrauftrag)**, the affiliated academic additionally holds the voting rights described in [Federated Legal Model](https://github.com/eebyak/OUG/blob/main/docs/governance/federated-legal-model.md) § 8(5)–(6), grounded in Grundordnung § 12 Absatz 1 Nummer 3 — subject to the open question flagged in § 8(6) of that framework, which should be confirmed before this Agreement represents the voting right as settled to the affiliate.

## § 5 Duties

The affiliated academic shall comply with OUG curriculum and examination standards, participate in required quality assurance and moderation procedures, complete required assessment training, disclose conflicts of interest, and uphold academic integrity standards.

## § 6 Academic Freedom

OUG guarantees academic freedom in accordance with Article 5(3) of the German Basic Law, applicable law, its own statutes, and the protections attached to the affiliate's primary appointment (Federated Legal Model § 9(2)). Participation in OUG programmes does not diminish research autonomy at the Home HEI.

## § 7 Conflict of Interest

The affiliated academic must disclose institutional or financial conflicts and recuse themselves from decisions where a material conflict exists.

## § 8 Termination

Affiliation may be terminated upon expiry of term, resignation, material breach of OUG standards, conflict-of-interest violation, or loss of eligibility, following due process under OUG statutes. Termination does not affect degrees already awarded based on the affiliate's prior contribution.

## § 9 Dispute Resolution

Disputes arising under this Agreement are routed in accordance with the [Conflict Escalation Framework](https://github.com/eebyak/OUG/blob/main/docs/governance/conflict-escalation-framework.md).
