> **What this document is.** The actual artifact that [DP-1 (The Designed Floor) and DP-12 (The Permission Floor)](design-pattern-register.md) describe — not a description of what such a document should do, but the document itself. Every governance rule in this repository that touches statute is classified here as one of three types: **Statutory Requirement** (the LHG or another external law mandates it), **Institutional Choice** (the law permits it; OUG chose it), or **Open Design Space** (the law permits it; OUG has not yet decided). The distinction matters because conflating the three is exactly how the Permission Illusion and the Compliance Ceiling — the two antipatterns this Map exists to prevent — actually happen: not through bad faith, but through nobody stopping to ask which of the three a given rule actually is.
> **Why this document doesn't start empty.** A Compliance Map with no entries invites exactly the failure it's meant to prevent — it looks like due diligence without doing any. This one is seeded with the three findings this session's own work actually produced, each one a real instance of checking primary statutory text rather than trusting institutional convention. New entries should be added the same way: by reading the LHG directly, not by inference from what "usually" holds in German higher education law.

## I. How to Read an Entry

Each entry states the governance rule, its classification, the specific statutory basis (paragraph and clause, not a general area of law), and — where it originated from this session's own work — a short account of what checking the primary text actually revealed, since the "how we found out" is often as instructive as the finding itself.

## II. Founding Entries

### Kooperationsvereinbarung Voting Rights

**Rule:** Academic staff engaged at OUG "im Rahmen von Kooperationsvereinbarungen" hold full active and passive Wahlrecht (Grundordnung § 12 Absatz 1 Nummer 3).

**Classification: Open Design Space, exercised as Institutional Choice.**

**Statutory basis:** § 9 Absatz 1 Satz 5 LHG grants membership to *"Hochschullehrerinnen und Hochschullehrer, die... im Rahmen von Kooperationsvereinbarungen dienstliche Aufgaben an der Hochschule wahrnehmen"* — this sentence sits outside the earlier list in § 9 Absatz 1 Satz 2, which is the only list the statute subjects to Grundordnung discretion over Wahlrecht. Read narrowly, the statute simply grants these members full standing without qualification.

**What checking primary text actually found:** this session initially assumed, by analogy with the weaker *kooptiert* category (§ 22 Absatz 4, Wahlrecht left to Grundordnung discretion, explicitly barred from becoming Dean), that federation academics would default to advisory status only. Reading § 9 Absatz 1 directly — not a summary of it — showed the Kooperationsvereinbarungen category is textually separate and was never subject to that same discretion-clause. The Grundordnung's own § 12 Absatz 1 Nummer 3 had already, independently, classified it correctly; this session's contribution was noticing the connection to the federation's Model B (Freistellungsvereinbarung) academics, not discovering a new legal fact. **One sub-question remains genuinely open, not resolved by this entry:** whether this Wahlrecht places a member inside the specific § 10 Absatz 1 Nummer 1 voting bloc used for Senate seat apportionment, which is textually limited to *hauptberuflich* staff. Flagged, not answered — see Federated Legal Model § 8(6).

### Kontaktstudium as the Legal Basis for Non-Degree Lifelong Learning

**Rule:** Non-degree, stackable learning participants are legally distinct from immatrikulierte Studierende and can be engaged, including through partner institutions outside the higher-education sector, without requiring full degree-track enrolment.

**Classification: Statutory Requirement (the category itself) enabling an Institutional Choice (how OUG uses it).**

**Statutory basis:** § 31 LHG (Weiterbildung) establishes Kontaktstudium as a category explicitly outside the Studiengang rules (§ 31 Absatz 5 Satz 2), resulting in a Zertifikat on completion, and — critically — deliverable *"auf Grund von Kooperationsvereinbarungen auch mit Einrichtungen außerhalb des Hochschulbereichs"* (§ 31 Absatz 5 Satz 1).

**What checking primary text actually found:** this entry directly enabled two separate design decisions that would otherwise have had no statutory footing: the permanent Community Circle seat for Kontaktstudium participants (Grundordnung § 5 Absatz 15) and the local co-presence mechanism in the Presence Architecture (Mechanism 4), since a Volkshochschule or company meeting room is exactly an "Einrichtung außerhalb des Hochschulbereichs" in the statute's own terms.

### The Kooptation / Dean Restriction

**Rule:** A co-opted (kooptiert) academic cannot be appointed Steward of Missions (Dean-equivalent) in that capacity.

**Classification: Statutory Requirement — a hard floor, not subject to Grundordnung discretion.**

**Statutory basis:** § 22 Absatz 4 Satz 3 LHG: *"Ein kooptiertes Mitglied kann als solches nicht zur Dekanin oder zum Dekan bestellt werden."*

**What checking primary text actually found:** this is the one entry in this founding set that constrains rather than enables — a genuine ceiling, not a floor to build on. It's included specifically because a Compliance Map that only records permissive findings would itself become a subtle form of the Compliance Ceiling antipattern: cataloguing freedom while quietly not recording the real limits.

## III. Open Design Space Not Yet Exercised

Entries the law appears to permit, flagged here as genuinely open rather than decided either way — the active agenda DP-1's counter-design specifically calls for, distinct from a passive record of past findings.

- **Contribution-weighted voting confined to a non-statutory federation body.** § 65c LHG's Duale Partner mechanism (one institutional vote per Dual Partner, regardless of size) shows German higher education law is not categorically opposed to institutional voting — it's tied to DHBW's specific dual-system architecture, which OUG does not share. Whether any comparable mechanism could be built for OUG's own partner federation, outside the Senate, remains unexamined rather than foreclosed.
- **The precise scope of § 9 Absatz 1 Satz 5's relationship to § 10 Absatz 1 Nummer 1** (see the Kooperationsvereinbarung entry above) — flagged as needing qualified legal review, not yet resolved.

## V. Ownership and Review

The Hochschulrat owns this Map, consistent with its role as constitutional steward (G1) and the body already responsible for the Rector's mid-term evaluation (Grundordnung § 4 Absatz 4). It is reviewed on the cycle specified in the [Governance Review Calendar](governance-review-calendar.md).

**The verification requirement is not left to individual diligence.** The [Amendment Procedure](amendment-procedure.md) requires that any proposed amendment asserting a legal constraint — any text of the form *"the law requires..."* or *"we cannot do X because of the LHG"* — cite the specific statutory paragraph and add or confirm the corresponding entry here before the amendment can proceed. This converts what would otherwise be a good habit, dependent on someone happening to check, into a gate the amendment process itself enforces.
