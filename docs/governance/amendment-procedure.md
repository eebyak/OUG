> **Scope:** this procedure governs amendment of the [Governance & Decision Framework](https://github.com/eebyak/OUG/blob/main/docs/governance/governance-architecture.md) specifically, and serves as the default amendment procedure for any other instrument in this repository that does not define its own. It does **not** govern the Strategic Constitution or the Grundordnung, both of which already have their own amendment clauses — see § 1 below.

## 1. What This Document Does Not Cover

Two documents in this repository already define their own amendment procedure, and this document does not duplicate or override either:

- **[Strategic Constitution](https://eebyak.github.io/OUG/strategy/strategic-constitution.html) § 8 (Status, Revision, and Evolution)** — defines the conditions for revision, the revision procedure, and the principle of constitutional stability. This is the governing procedure for the Strategic Constitution.
- **[Grundordnung](https://github.com/eebyak/OUG/blob/main/docs/governance/grundordnung.md) § 16 Absatz 1** — Anlagen 1 and 2 are amended "durch Satzung zu ihrer Änderung," i.e. through the Grundordnung's own statutory amendment process. The Grundordnung's footer records that its own adoption required Senat and Hochschulrat resolutions; amendment follows the same joint procedure.

This document exists because the Governance & Decision Framework — unlike those two — does not yet have its own amendment clause, despite describing itself in several places as binding. It closes that gap.

## 2. Conditions for Amendment

Amendment of the Governance & Decision Framework is appropriate where:

1. the Institutional Mapping (§ 2.5 of that document) no longer reflects the actual bodies established by the Grundordnung, following a Grundordnung revision;
2. a Decision Domain (§ 3) is found to be misassigned, ambiguous, or missing, through accumulated experience or an unresolved Competence-Boundary Review (see the [Conflict Escalation Framework](https://github.com/eebyak/OUG/blob/main/docs/governance/conflict-escalation-framework.md) Route 2);
3. the Federated Legal Model or another repository instrument changes in a way that affects a flagged dependency (e.g. Mission Hub membership, § 5.4);
4. a participation or coupling mechanism (§§ 4 and 6) proves unenforceable or is repeatedly bypassed in practice.

Following the Strategic Constitution's own principle of stability (§ 8.4), amendment should not be motivated by short-term convenience, a single unresolved disagreement, or a wish to avoid a difficult application of the existing framework. Where a conflict recurs, the [Conflict Escalation Framework](https://github.com/eebyak/OUG/blob/main/docs/governance/conflict-escalation-framework.md) should be used first; only where the escalation framework itself reveals that the underlying assignment is wrong does amendment become appropriate.

## 3. Procedure

**Step 1 — Proposal.** Any body named in the Institutional Mapping (§ 2.5 of the Governance & Decision Framework) or any recognised participatory body (§ 4.3 of that document) may propose an amendment. The proposal must identify the specific section(s) affected and the reason amendment is sought, by reference to § 2 above.

**Step 2 — Preparation and routing.** The Rektorat (G2) prepares the proposal for review, consolidating it with any related proposals and identifying which decision domains, principles, or dependencies (§ 5.4, § 7) are affected.

**Step 3 — Constitutional alignment review.** The Hochschulrat (G1) reviews the proposal for alignment with the Strategic Constitution and the Grundordnung, in line with its role as Constitutional Steward (Governance Architecture § 5.7, § 8.3). Where the proposal touches matters reserved to the Strategic Constitution itself (its First Principles, Pillars, or precedence rules), it must instead follow the Strategic Constitution's own § 8 procedure, and this procedure does not apply.

**Step 4 — Senate consultation.** Where the proposal affects Academic and Quality Governance (Domains D-D, D-E, D-F), the Senat is consulted before adoption, consistent with its statutory academic authority (Grundordnung § 5 Absatz 1).

**Step 5 — Adoption.** The amendment is adopted by joint resolution of the Senat and the Hochschulrat, mirroring the procedure by which the Grundordnung itself was adopted (Grundordnung, closing clause) and consistent with this framework's role in guiding the interpretation of statutes for both bodies.

**Step 6 — Documentation and versioning.** Adopted amendments are dated, versioned, and recorded, together with the reasoning under § 2. The Institutional Mapping table and the Decision-Rights Matrix are updated in the same revision where the amendment affects them.

**Step 7 — Effective date.** The amendment states its own effective date and any transition provisions for decisions already in progress under the prior version.

## 4. Relationship to Other Amendment Procedures

| Instrument | Amendment procedure | Governing clause |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strategic Constitution | Own procedure | Strategic Constitution § 8 |
| Grundordnung (including Anlagen 1–2) | Satzungsänderung; Senat + Hochschulrat resolution | Grundordnung § 16 Absatz 1 |
| Governance & Decision Framework | This procedure | This document |
| Conflict Escalation Framework, Decision-Rights Matrix | This procedure, by extension, unless and until each defines its own | This document, § 1 |
| Federated Legal Model | Not yet defined here — see [Document Status](https://github.com/eebyak/OUG/blob/main/docs/index.md#document-status) | *(open item)* |

## 5. Status

This procedure is internally authoritative in the sense defined in Governance Architecture § 0. It applies to future amendments of that document from the date of its own adoption under § 3 above.
