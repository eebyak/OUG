## T4INF1601 · Scientific Programming & Experimentation

*Data, AI & Intelligent Systems*

### At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **ECTS** | 5 |
| **Total workload** | 150 hours |
| **Contact time** | 72 hours |
| **Self-study** | 78 hours |
| **Year** | 1 |
| **Level** | Bachelor (EQF 6) |
| **Duration** | 3 months |
| **Delivery** | Online |
| **Languages** | English, German |
| **Assessment** | Combined assessment — programming project / implementation (standard grading) |

### Module overview

Programming language concepts; programming in an appropriate language (variables, data types, control structures, data structures, modularisation); scientific computing with appropriate tools; data preprocessing; statistical analysis; data visualisation; responsible use of AI-assisted tools in data analysis.

### Learning outcomes

#### Analysis & Problem Solving

*Competences in analysing problems and developing solution approaches*

- **Analysing problem statements** — *Advanced analysis*
  Independently analyse data analysis problems and identify their relevant requirements.

#### Application Development

*Competences in applying concepts and developing applications*

- **Using tools** — *Understanding & application*
  Use appropriate development environments and scientific computing tools.
- **Using AI tools** — *Understanding & application*
  Use AI-assisted tools to support data analysis and scientific computing tasks.

#### Communication

*Competences in communicating, presenting and exchanging professional views*

- **Presenting results** — *Understanding & application*
  Present data analysis results orally and document them in written form.
- **Exchanging views with experts** — *Understanding & application*
  Discuss and critically question analysis results within a team and with domain representatives.

#### Knowledge & Understanding

*Competences in subject knowledge, foundational understanding and conceptual coherence*

- **Knowing foundational concepts** — *Foundation*
  Know fundamental concepts of programming languages and their use in data analysis.
- **Knowing terms and roles** — *Foundation*
  Know appropriate tools and environments for scientific computing and data analysis.
- **Understanding interdependencies** — *Understanding & application*
  Understand the fundamental steps of a data analysis pipeline: data collection, preprocessing, statistical analysis, and visualisation.

#### Methodological Competences

*Competences in selecting and applying appropriate methods and process models*

- **Applying methods** — *Understanding & application*
  Apply statistical methods for data analysis and visualisation.

#### Personal Development

*Competences in self-development, critical reflection and autonomous working*

- **Working independently** — *Understanding & application*
  Independently orient themselves in new programming languages and scientific tools.

#### Programming & Development

*Competences in software development, programming and professional tooling*

- **Implementing solutions** — *Understanding & application*
  Implement data analysis solutions in an appropriate programming language.
- **Developing software** — *Evaluation & creation*
  Independently or as a team, plan, implement, and deliver a complete data analysis project — from data collection through analysis to visualisation and presentation.

#### Research & Academic Work

*Competences in academic research methods and scientific writing*

- **Evaluating AI systems as research objects** — *Evaluation & creation*
  Check and compare data analysis results and AI-generated outputs using appropriate metrics.

#### Software Architecture & Design

*Competences in designing architectures and systems*

- **Designing solutions** — *Understanding & application*
  Structure and modularise programming projects for data analysis appropriately.

#### Teamwork

*Competences in teamwork, collaboration and team building*

- **Contributing to teams** — *Understanding & application*
  Work in teams on data analysis projects and distribute tasks effectively.

### Recommended literature and resources

- McKinney, W.: Python for Data Analysis (3rd ed., 2022), O'Reilly
- VanderPlas, J.: Python Data Science Handbook (2022), O'Reilly [also free online]
- Wickham, H. / Grolemund, G.: R for Data Science (2nd ed., 2023), O'Reilly [free online]
- Géron, A.: Hands-On Machine Learning with Scikit-Learn, Keras & TensorFlow (3rd ed., 2022), O'Reilly
- Strang, G.: Linear Algebra and Learning from Data (2019), Wellesley Cambridge Press [further reading]

| **Delivery** | Online |

### Module overview

Unit 1 (Algorithms): Fundamental concepts and algorithms for intelligent systems; bionic foundations; evolutionary algorithms; regressor and classifier; introduction to neural networks. Unit 2 (Ethics & Law): Foundations of ethics in CS; compliance; GDPR; criminal law; civil liability; copyright; patent law; societal effects of AI systems.

### Learning outcomes

#### Application Development

*Competences in applying concepts and developing applications*

- **Using AI tools** — *Understanding & application*
  Use AI-assisted tools to support the design and implementation of intelligent systems.
- **Structuring and optimising prompts for LLMs** — *Understanding & application*
  Structure and optimise prompts for AI language models to produce high-quality, task-appropriate results.

#### Ethics

*Competences in ethical reasoning and responsible conduct*

- **Recognising AI bias and fairness issues** — *Understanding & application*
  Apply ethical criteria to recognise and name bias, fairness issues, and transparency gaps in AI systems.
- **Analysing AI's societal effects** — *Advanced analysis*
  Analyse the structural effects of AI systems on society, labour markets, and legal frameworks.
- **Reflecting on AI and social justice** — *Evaluation & creation*
  Analyse how AI systems can reproduce or amplify existing social inequalities and derive initial consequences for professional conduct.

#### Knowledge & Understanding

*Competences in subject knowledge, foundational understanding and conceptual coherence*

- **Knowing foundational concepts** — *Foundation*
  Know fundamental concepts of intelligent systems: regressor, classifier, evolutionary algorithms, and bionic foundations.
- **Knowing terms and roles** — *Foundation*
  Know the foundational concepts of ethics in computer science and their significance for the development and use of digital systems.
- **Knowing the foundational principles of AI systems** — *Foundation*
  Know the foundational principles of AI systems — including machine learning, neural networks, and evolutionary algorithms — and their capabilities and limitations.

#### Methodological Competences

*Competences in selecting and applying appropriate methods and process models*

- **Selecting methods** — *Understanding & application*
  Select appropriate algorithms and methods from the field of intelligent systems for a given problem.
- **Applying methods** — *Understanding & application*
  Appropriately take into account legal and ethical requirements — in particular data protection and compliance — when developing intelligent systems.
- **Transferring theoretical knowledge to complex real-world contexts** — *Advanced analysis*
  Systematically incorporate legal and ethical requirements into the design and evaluation of intelligent systems.

#### Personal Development

*Competences in self-development, critical reflection and autonomous working*

- **Working independently** — *Understanding & application*
  Independently orient themselves in new concepts and tools from the field of intelligent systems.
- **Making independent decisions about AI use** — *Understanding & application*
  Make reasoned, independent decisions about when and how to use AI tools — and when human judgement remains indispensable.

#### Programming & Development

*Competences in software development, programming and professional tooling*

- **Implementing solutions** — *Understanding & application*
  Implement simple intelligent systems based on a given design specification.
- **Developing software** — *Evaluation & creation*
  Independently design, implement, and evaluate a simple intelligent system for a given problem.

#### Software Architecture & Design

*Competences in designing architectures and systems*

- **Designing solutions** — *Understanding & application*
  Design simple intelligent systems for a given problem.

### Recommended literature and resources

- [Unit_Algorithms] Russell, S. / Norvig, P.: Artificial Intelligence — A Modern Approach (4th ed., 2020), Pearson
- [Unit_Algorithms] Géron, A.: Hands-On Machine Learning with Scikit-Learn, Keras & TensorFlow (3rd ed., 2022), O'Reilly
- [Unit_Algorithms] Mitchell, M.: Artificial Intelligence: A Guide for Thinking Humans (2019), Pelican
- [Unit_Ethics_Law] European Parliament and Council: EU AI Act — Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (2024)
- [Unit_Ethics_Law] Zweig, K.: Ein Algorithmus hat kein Taktgefühl (2019), Heyne
- [Unit_Ethics_Law] Mittelstadt, B. et al.: The Ethics of Algorithms, Big Data & Society (2016)
- [Unit_Ethics_Law] Bundesdatenschutzbeauftragter: DSGVO Kurzpapiere (current), bfdi.bund.de
- [Unit_Ethics_Law] Floridi, L. et al.: An Ethical Framework for a Good AI Society, AI4People (2018)

## T4INF1003 · Theoretical Computer Science II — Algorithms and Complexity

|---|---|

| **Level** | Bachelor (EQF 6) |
| **Duration** | 3 months |
| **Delivery** | Online |
| **Languages** | English, German |
| **Assessment** | Written examination (standard grading) |

### Module overview

Introduction to algorithms; complexity theory: O-notation, complexity classes (P, NP, NP-complete); search and sorting algorithms, hashing; correctness of algorithms; data structures: sets, lists, stacks, queues; trees, binary search trees, balanced graphs and graph algorithms; coding: compression, error-detecting and error-correcting codes.

### Learning outcomes

#### Algorithms & Data Structures

*Competences in algorithm design, data structures and complexity analysis*

- **Analysing complexity** — *Advanced analysis*
  Analyse the complexity of algorithms for key problem classes in computer science — using O-notation and complexity classes (P, NP, NP-complete) — and assess their practical applicability in professional contexts.
- **Designing algorithms** — *Evaluation & creation*
  Design algorithms for defined problem classes — including search and sorting algorithms, hashing, and graph algorithms — justify their correctness, and adapt efficient data structures to concrete practical problems.

#### Analysis & Problem Solving

*Competences in analysing problems and developing solution approaches*

- **Classifying information** — *Understanding & application*
  Classify information and data structures according to their properties and algorithmic requirements — sets, lists, trees, graphs — and derive justified decisions about the appropriate choice of data structure.
- **Defining AI‑specific success metrics** — *Advanced analysis*
  Assess the necessity of a complexity analysis for a programme and select appropriate metrics for professional deployment — a competence directly transferable to evaluating AI system performance and machine learning model behaviour.

### Recommended literature and resources

- Cormen, T. H., Leiserson, C. E., Rivest, R. L., & Stein, C. (2022). Introduction to Algorithms (4th ed.). MIT Press.
- Sedgewick, R., & Wayne, K. (2011). Algorithms (4th ed.). Addison-Wesley.
- Wirth, N. (2000). Algorithmen und Datenstrukturen. Teubner.
